Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

The short version

Puzzleverse doesn’t collect, share or sell any personal information. There are no ads, trackers, analytics or accounts.

What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, best times and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Puzzleverse Plus is sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether Plus is active on your device. Apple’s handling of purchases is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

Puzzleverse is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third-party services

Puzzleverse contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. The two open-source libraries it uses (PuzzleMaker and Vortex) run entirely on your device and do not send any data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Visit the support page.
